Our Identity & Purpose

One Accountable Partner Between Your Brand and Bangladesh's Best Factories

Stitch Meridian was established to solve the core challenges international fashion brands face when manufacturing in Bangladesh: inconsistent communication, hidden costs, and quality variance.

Origin & Mission

Bridging the Gap with Radical Transparency

Bangladesh is one of the world's leading apparel exporters, home to state-of-the-art LEED-certified green factories and unmatched craftsmanship across knit, woven, denim, and outerwear. Yet, managing overseas production remotely can feel unpredictable without trusted eyes and ears on the factory floor.

We founded Stitch Meridian to act as an authentic extension of your own merchandising and technical team. We do not operate as passive middlemen who simply pass messages along. We actively verify yarn availability, calculate open-book costing, conduct daily on-site inline inspections, and safeguard your brand's quality standards before goods ever leave Dhaka.

Our Core Commitments

100% On-Site QC OversightIndependent inspections at fabric intake, during sewing, and final AQL 2.5 checks.
Transparent Costing SheetsClear visibility into fabric, trim, making charges (CM), and logistics breakdown.
Verified Compliance OnlyPartnering exclusively with vetted factories holding active social and safety certifications.
Guiding Principles

Our Approach to Quality and Ethics

We believe sustainable business is built on uncompromising ethical standards and rigorous technical discipline.

Pillar 01

Worker Welfare & Safety

We inspect and confirm that all partner factories adhere strictly to Bangladesh labor laws, international safety standards (RSC/Accord), fair wages, and humane working environments.

Pillar 02

Environmental Accountability

We actively champion water-efficient dyeing techniques, organic and BCI cotton sourcing, OEKO-TEX Standard 100 chemical safety, and sustainable eco-washing in our denim lines.

Pillar 03

Zero False Claims

We never inflate factory capacities, invent certifications, or make unrealistic delivery promises. If a production risk or bottleneck arises, we report it immediately with solutions.

Dhaka Operational Base

Based in Dhaka, Working Worldwide

Operating directly from Dhaka (Dhaka, Bangladesh) places our team within short driving distance of Bangladesh's key textile manufacturing hubs—including Gazipur, Narayanganj, Savar, Ashulia, and the export ports of Chattogram.

Our merchandisers communicate seamlessly across time zones, aligning daily updates with European, British, and North American business hours so you never wait days for an answer.
