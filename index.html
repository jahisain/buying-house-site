Structured Execution

How We Work

A transparent, accountable six-step sourcing process from initial tech pack review to port departure.

01Step 1 of 6

Brief and tech pack

What you provide:

Tech packs, size specs, target quantities, fabric preferences and target delivery date.

What we deliver:

Initial technical feasibility assessment, factory capacity matching and projected milestone timeline.

02Step 2 of 6

Factory matching and costing

What you provide:

Target pricing guidelines and buyer compliance standards.

What we deliver:

Vetted partner factory options, yarn/fabric availability check and transparent itemized cost sheet.

03Step 3 of 6

Sampling and approval

What you provide:

Fit comments, measurement corrections, lab dip approvals and trim feedback.

What we deliver:

Proto samples, fit samples, lab dips, strike-offs and pre-production (PP) samples.

04Step 4 of 6

Production with in-line QC

What you provide:

Final PO sign-off and LC / payment confirmation.

What we deliver:

Raw fabric inspection (4-point system), cutting checks, continuous in-line QC and weekly photo progress updates.

05Step 5 of 6

Final inspection

What you provide:

Confirmation of buyer inspection criteria and AQL standard.

What we deliver:

AQL 2.5 final random inspection report, measurement audit and packaging integrity verification.

06Step 6 of 6

Shipping and documentation

What you provide:

Forwarder nomination and destination consignee documentation details.

What we deliver:

Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, export clearance and on-board vessel tracking.
