Vetted Production Infrastructure

Our Manufacturing Network

We connect international buyers with pre-vetted, socially compliant factories in Bangladesh. Each partner is selected exclusively for their specialized category capability, machine infrastructure, and verified safety record.

Quality & Safety Assurance

Our 5-Pillar Factory Vetting Standard

Before we place any sampling or bulk order with a factory, our Dhaka technical compliance team conducts an extensive on-site audit against these strict criteria.

Social & Ethical Compliance Audits

Active, verified certifications from international auditing bodies (BSCI minimum Grade C, SEDEX SMETA 4-Pillar, or WRAP). Strict enforcement of minimum wage laws, overtime regulations, and zero tolerance for child or forced labor.

Structural, Fire & Electrical Safety

Full certification under the RMG Sustainability Council (RSC) and Accord structural guidelines. Verified building blueprints, functional emergency exits, automated sprinkler systems, and regular fire evacuation drills.

Modern Machinery & Technical Setup

Audited equipment maintenance logs, automatic spreading machines, digital needle detectors on all packaging exits, and dedicated sample rooms with pattern-grading CAD software (Gerber / Lectra).

In-House Laboratory & Testing Facilities

On-site physical testing labs capable of evaluating color fastness (washing, perspiration, rubbing), dimensional stability, fabric weight (GSM), and button/snap pull testing (90N standard).

Financial Stability & Direct Traceability

Established banking relationships and transparent material purchasing histories with certified yarn and fabric mills to prevent unexpected mid-production financial freezes.

Industrial Hubs

Capability Overview by Category

To protect partner agreements, specific factory names are disclosed directly to buyers upon commercial matching and NDA confirmation.

Knitwear Network

Hub Locations: Gazipur, Narayanganj & Savar
Typical partner facility: 150,000 to 500,000 pcs / month
Technical Setup & Equipment

High-speed multi-gauge circular knitting machines, automated computer-controlled cutting tables, in-house continuous bleaching and softflow dyeing, and high-volume screen printing units.

Verified Audit Standards

BSCI, OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GOTS, SEDEX SMETA

Woven Garments Network

Hub Locations: Ashulia, Savar & Gazipur
Typical partner facility: 80,000 to 250,000 pcs / month
Technical Setup & Equipment

Automated collar and cuff profile stitchers, multi-head embroidery machines, computerized pattern markers, and strict interlining fusing peel-strength testing.

Verified Audit Standards

BSCI, WRAP, SEDEX SMETA, ISO 9001

Denim & Jeans Network

Hub Locations: Dhaka Industrial Zone & Chattogram
Typical partner facility: 100,000 to 300,000 pcs / month
Technical Setup & Equipment

Sustainable eco-laundry plants equipped with Tonello and Jeanologia laser marking systems, ozone bleaching chambers, nano-bubble technology, and heavy-duty automatic pocket set sewers.

Verified Audit Standards

BSCI, OEKO-TEX, ZDHC Level 3 Wastewater, HIGG Index

Sweaters Network

Hub Locations: Gazipur, Mymensingh & Konabari
Typical partner facility: 60,000 to 200,000 pcs / month
Technical Setup & Equipment

Fully computerized flat knitting machines (Shima Seiki and Stoll 3GG to 14GG), precision linking stations, industrial dry-cleaning and yarn conditioning ovens.

Verified Audit Standards

BSCI, WRAP, SEDEX, GRS (Global Recycled Standard)

Technical Outerwear Network

Hub Locations: Dhaka Export Processing Zone (DEPZ) & Savar
Typical partner facility: 40,000 to 120,000 pcs / month
Technical Setup & Equipment

Hot air seam-sealing taping equipment, ultrasonic quilting, automated down-injection chambers, hydrostatic water-pressure tester, and clean-room assembly lines.

Verified Audit Standards

BSCI, ISO 14001, OEKO-TEX Step, RSC Structural Approved
